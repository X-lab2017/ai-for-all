ONE / SHARED VISION

One Shared Vision

Make AI affordable and useful.

Lower the cost of access and use.
Build understanding, judgment and creative ability.

For developers

Help learners build skills, builders create applications, and engineers keep systems reliable.

Three complementary perspectives on people-centered AI for all.

TWO / MUTUAL REINFORCEMENT

Two Reinforcing Forces

Openness lowers barriers. Creativity expands possibilities.

Open source

Clear licenses, accessible documentation, reusable work and ongoing maintenance enable participation.

AI capabilities→Digital public goods→AI for All

Openness supports creativity. Creativity enriches openness.

THREE / HOW IT WORKS

Three Flywheels

Data informs judgment. Talent drives creation. Value sustains investment.

Connect evidence, capabilities and resources.
Let each round of practice support the next.

01 Data flywheelInform evaluation and decisions ↗02 Talent flywheelTurn resources into capabilities and outcomes ↗03 Value flywheelSecure support for continued creation ↗
THREE / FLYWHEEL 01

Data flywheel

Ground evaluation in evidence.

Collect data · OpenDigger

Continuously gather data and indicators from open-source practice, preserving traceable evidence.

↻ Evaluation feedback improves the next round of data collection.

Back to the three flywheels ↗
THREE / FLYWHEEL 02

Talent flywheel

Make contributions visible and growth recognized.

Assess contributions

Recognize code, documentation, testing, teaching, translation, maintenance and community collaboration.

↻ New contributions and outcomes feed the next evaluation.

Back to the three flywheels ↗
THREE / FLYWHEEL 03

Value flywheel

Make the returns on investment verifiable.

Invest resources

Companies and institutions offer model credits, funding, jobs and practical opportunities, with clear responsibilities.

↻ Verified outcomes inform the next round of investment.

Back to the three flywheels ↗

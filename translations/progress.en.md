# Progress

We keep public records of our foundations, current actions and next steps.

## Practical foundations

### OpenDigger · Open-source data and metrics

An open-source data analysis project initiated by X-lab, providing data and metrics for understanding developer collaboration, projects and communities.

[Repository](https://github.com/X-lab2017/open-digger) · [Project website](https://open-digger.cn/)

### OpenRank · Collaboration-network evaluation

OpenRank methods examine contribution and influence through collaboration networks. We attend to interpretation, context and limitations, using varied evidence to understand contributions.

[OpenRank methods](https://open-digger.cn/blog/2025-08-09-openrank-introduction)

### OpenShare · Developer ecosystem

OpenShare explores connections among contributions, developer capabilities, organizational needs and resource returns. Participation and available resources follow the platform’s current terms.

[Visit OpenShare](https://open-share.com/)

### Global open-source observation · Reports and research

The preview edition of the 2026 Global Open Source Development Report is public. Its release note also describes future work on the OSIDX Open Source Development Index. BenchCouncil’s evaluation science research provides a public reference for evaluation objects, methods and evidence.

[Report preview release](https://open-digger.cn/blog/2026-07-23-global-open-source-development-report) · [BenchCouncil Evaluatology](https://www.benchcouncil.org/Evaluatology/)

These are practical foundations and research references. Each project’s scope, permissions and status follow its public documentation. They do not by themselves demonstrate inclusion outcomes for this initiative.

## 001 · Open manifesto co-creation

**Status: open and ongoing.** Launched on 8 October 2026 through [Issue #1](https://github.com/X-lab2017/ai-for-all/issues/1). No prior contribution or score is required.

**Delivered:** the Chinese manifesto, English translation, website, participation guide, issue templates, and five interactive diagrams with static downloads. A [ten-chapter interactive presentation (Chinese)](https://www.x-lab.info/ai-for-all/presentation/), official assets for five open-model families, a 90-second silent tour, and chapter-by-chapter speaker notes are now available.

**This revision:** two rounds of proposals and the [expert review in Issue #2](https://github.com/X-lab2017/ai-for-all/issues/2#issuecomment-6071564956) shaped the DPG pathway, two reinforcing cycles and three perspectives on inclusion. Each of the five elements has an interactive diagram. v1.1 was published on 9 October 2026.

**Next:** collect feedback on concepts, practical needs and diagram usability. Record responses through issues, pull requests and the changelog.

## Further actions

Learning resources, developer practice and resource support are candidate directions. Each action will identify participants, conditions, available resources, responsibility and ways to examine outcomes before being announced in its own issue.

Growth targets will follow defined baselines, timeframes and measurement methods. Resource eligibility follows each project’s announcements.

We aim to review progress quarterly, explaining changes, limitations and adjustments, using public evidence and feedback shared with consent.

# Participate in AI for All

People of different backgrounds and levels of experience are welcome. You do not need an existing project, a contribution score or paid membership to begin.

## Choose a starting point

- **Feedback on the manifesto:** identify the passage, explain your concern and suggest a change.
- **A practical action:** explain who you hope to help, the problem they face and the work you propose.
- **Experience from use:** describe the setting, the changes you observed and the problems that remain.

Check existing Issues before opening a new one. You can add to an existing discussion or use the English participation template. You do not need to complete every field at once.

[Browse discussions](https://github.com/X-lab2017/ai-for-all/issues) · [Start in English](https://github.com/X-lab2017/ai-for-all/issues/new?template=04-participate-en.md) · [Launch materials](https://www.x-lab.info/ai-for-all/launch/)

## Contribute a change

The Chinese README.md is authoritative; translations/en.md is maintained alongside it. Explain changes in a Pull Request and check both languages. Discuss changes to core principles in an Issue before merging, and record accepted changes in CHANGELOG.md.

The website is generated from the manifesto and progress documents using `python3 scripts/build_site.py`. Edit those sources rather than the generated home pages. The release hub is maintained under `site/launch/`.

## Respect participants

Focus discussion on ideas and actions. Share only material you have permission to share. Do not include private personal information, credentials, confidential partnership details or other sensitive material in public Issues. See [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md) for content and brand permissions.

An action proposal is not a promise that a project or resource allocation has been approved. Maintainers will consider needs, available resources and feasibility, then clarify status, responsibility and support conditions before an action begins.

Public evidence and feedback should distinguish existing foundations, plans, pilots and completed work. Evaluation supports understanding and improvement; a score cannot represent a person's full worth or automatically establish an inclusion outcome.
